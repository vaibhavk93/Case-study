How this register was built, and how to read it

Every source here was consulted during the writing of this guide. The Verified column is honest about something that matters:

  • ✓ Live — the URL was fetched during the research pass for this guide and returned the content described. 29 of the 54 sources below.
  • Canonical — a well-known, stable URL for a source whose content is relied on, but which was not fetched during this build because direct outbound requests were blocked by the build environment's network policy. These are high-confidence references, not verified links. Confirm before citing one in anything consequential.

Two books (Minto's Pyramid Principle, Rumelt's Good Strategy Bad Strategy) have no canonical free URL and are listed with a publisher or reference page.

What was deliberately not done

No quotations have been invented, no statistics have been attributed to sources that do not contain them, and no company's internal practice is described as fact unless the source is that company. Where this guide states a number — market sizes, conversion rates, cost structures in worked cases — it is either labelled as a public approximation or explicitly invented for arithmetic, as stated in each case. Several widely repeated interview-prep claims were left out because no credible source for them could be found.
